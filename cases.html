Case 01 / Local growth

Harbor Coffee

A local cafe needed more student visits without sounding like another discount ad.

Brief

Local coffee shop, low budget, weak proof, TikTok and Reels.

Diagnosis

Sell an organic validation sprint before any retainer. Proof needed before volume.

Generated pack

Landing page, 12 content topics, student offer scripts, quote, and approval workflow.

Test

Compared product-only hooks against comment-reply local proof hooks.

Renewal logic

Continue because saves and replies rose around local proof, not discount-only posts.

Case 02 / AI video studio

NovaClips AI

An AI short drama studio needed to turn viral curiosity into a productized service.

Brief

AI mini drama, unclear offer, growing maturity, comment-to-episode trend.

Diagnosis

Clarify one audience, one promise, one proof asset, and one CTA before producing.

Generated pack

Website preview, Skill OS, creative matrix, episode hooks, script, and sprint quote.

Test

Tested founder CTA, workflow proof, and audience-comment episode hooks.

Renewal logic

Continue if comment quality and founder DMs improve, then package a monthly episode system.

Case 03 / Premium UGC

GlowBar Studio

A beauty UGC operator needed premium positioning instead of trend-chasing noise.

Brief

Beauty UGC, premium positioning, strong visuals, needs better proof packaging.

Diagnosis

Reduce noisy trend language. Lead with taste, proof, calm authority, and pricing logic.

Generated pack

Editorial landing copy, creator brief, proof map, case structure, and quote.

Test

Compared transformation proof, creator POV, and customer ritual hooks.

Renewal logic

Renewal is justified if premium proof reduces sales friction and raises qualified inquiries.
