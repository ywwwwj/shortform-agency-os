FAQBefore beta

Reduce hesitation

Questions buyers will ask before paying.

Is this a SaaS?

Not yet. The current beta is a static/local platform prototype. The goal is to validate repeated client usage before building login, database, and paid SaaS plans.

Can GPT or Doubao do this?

They can generate copy and remember long chats. This product structures the work into client profile, AI handoff, approval log, learning log, output layer, and renewal report.

Does it have built-in AI?

The current version uses BYO-AI mode: it creates structured prompts and schemas for ChatGPT, Claude, or your preferred tool. API-connected AI comes after validation.

How will templates stay fresh?

The platform uses a weekly trend refresh workflow: source social signals, extract the reusable mechanic, check client fit and risk, then update the workspace template fields.

What can users export?

Standalone website HTML, Markdown project pack, delivery packs, and renewal advisor report from the workspace.

Who is it for?

Indie operators, UGC agencies, AI video studios, short-form content freelancers, and people productizing client delivery.

What should be improved next?

Real customer examples, a Framer/Webflow-native version, better marketplace screenshots, and checkout integration.
