Method

Models can change. Brand context and learning should compound.

1Structured memory

Brand Brain separates positioning, audience, offer, voice, proof, products, prohibited claims, competitors, winning patterns, and examples.

2Content projects

Each draft keeps its generation context, output, versions, revision reasons, publication state, and manually entered results.

3Explainable AI

Every AI output must show memory used, why the angle was chosen, assumptions, risks, and proposed updates.

4Human approval

AI never writes permanent Brand Memory on its own. Users approve, modify, or reject every proposed update.
